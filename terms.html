4. Games and simulations use pretend money

The apple stall and the app's other games and simulations are teaching tools.

  • They use pretend money only. No real money, account, order or trade is ever involved.
  • Prices, customers, other traders and news events are generated by the app. They are not real market data.
  • The rules are simplified. Real markets include things the games leave out or simplify, such as fees, taxes, market rules, liquidity and the pressure of risking real money.
  • Simulated results, profits, losses and scores are hypothetical. They don't show what you would earn or lose in a real market and they don't predict real-market returns. Past results, whether real or simulated, never guarantee future results.
  • Your desk score measures how you ran the simulation (for example, managing risk and reacting to news), not your ability to invest or trade real money.

8. Your progress and data

In this version of the app, your progress (XP, streak, ranks, completed lessons and review dates) is saved only in your web browser's storage on your own device. Your written answers are checked inside the app on your device. We don't receive this information, and the app has no accounts, sign-in or advertising trackers.

  • Progress can be lost. It is deleted if you clear your browser's data or use private browsing, and it doesn't move to another browser or device.
  • iPhone and iPad. Safari may delete a website's saved data after about 7 days of using Safari without opening that website, unless the website has been added to your Home Screen. Opening the app at least once a week, or adding it to your Home Screen, helps keep your progress.
  • Moving your progress. "Move my progress" in the About menu (ⓘ) turns your saved progress, including any written debriefs, into a progress code that you can paste into another browser, device or the Home Screen app. The code is created on your device and goes only where you paste it. Anyone you give it to can see what it contains.
  • Offline copy. To work offline and from your Home Screen, the app keeps a copy of its own files (pages, art and fonts) in your browser. This copy contains none of your information.
  • Reset. "Reset progress" in the About menu deletes your saved progress from that browser. It can't be undone.
  • No backup. We can't recover lost progress, because we never receive it.

If a later version adds accounts or collects personal information, we will publish a Privacy Policy explaining what we collect and why, and ask for any consent the law requires, before that happens.
